    SEASON PASS

    · · free for everyone

    Every bit of XP you earn in matches also fills the pass: 1,000 XP a tier. Ten tiers give you something from this season's theme to keep (equip it in Customize); the rest give 250 bonus XP. A new season starts each month with the next theme, and each theme comes back every four months.

    ACCOUNT

    Your player ID —

    Your account is everything this browser has saved: your name, settings, crosshair, loadout, key bindings, career stats and training-range best. There's no sign-up. Back it up as an account code and paste it into any browser to carry on there.

    Back up

    Your code changes as you play, so back up again after a session to keep newer progress. Keep it to yourself: anyone with it can load your account.

    Import

    Importing swaps this browser over to that account and reloads.

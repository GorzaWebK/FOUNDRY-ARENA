# Changelog

## 1.0.0 (November 2026)

The first full release.

- **Modes:** Domination, Search & Destroy and Infected join Deathmatch, Team
  Deathmatch, King of the Hill, Capture the Flag, Gun Game, Last One Standing and
  Snipers Only.
- **Bots:** they flank, call out what they see to teammates, peek from low cover,
  pre-aim and play every objective. New Veteran difficulty.
- **Weapons:** burst rifle, marksman rifle and revolver (a second sidearm choice).
- **Gunsmith:** four attachment slots per gun, unlocked by kills with that gun.
- **Camos:** unlocked one at a time, every 50 kills with a gun (Woodland 50,
  Desert 100, Arctic 150, Crimson 200, Tiger 250); Gold needs 300 kills.
- **Medals and achievements:** 35 medals, 33 achievements, nine exclusive
  calling cards.
- **Season pass:** free for everyone, monthly, 30 tiers and four rotating themes.
- **Audio:** live-synthesised menu and adaptive match music, per-map ambience,
  music and effects volume.
- **Graphics:** Low / Medium / High / Ultra, render scale, dynamic resolution,
  a frame rate cap and reduced camera motion. About a third fewer draw calls.
- **Online:** host migration (a match carries on when the host leaves), chat
  filter, mute, report and kick, flood and spam limits, verified ranked results.
- **Launch polish:** loading screen, first-run setup (name, graphics picked for
  your computer, tutorial), credits, version number, link previews and store art.
